# lid.176.ftz

fastText's language identification model, compressed version (176 languages), from
<https://fasttext.cc/docs/en/language-identification.html>
(`https://dl.fbaipublicfiles.com/fasttext/supervised-models/lid.176.ftz`, SHA-256
`8f3472cfe8738a7b6099e8e999c3cbfae0dcd15696aac7d7738a8039db603e83`), unmodified.

A. Joulin, E. Grave, P. Bojanowski, T. Mikolov, *Bag of Tricks for Efficient Text Classification*, 2016;
A. Joulin, E. Grave, P. Bojanowski, M. Douze, H. Jégou, T. Mikolov, *FastText.zip: Compressing text
classification models*, 2016.

`language.py` reads it (AD-093).
